# Glossary

**Onboarding request**
A prospect's request to get started with Datorix, made by submitting the onboarding request form. It is the site's single conversion.

**Onboarding request form**
The page where a prospect gives the details needed to follow up on a onboarding request, and consents to being contacted.

**Prospect**
A person at an organization who has not yet used Datorix and submits a onboarding request.

**Sales inbox**
The company mailbox where onboarding requests arrive and where the team replies to prospects.

**Privacy consent**
A prospect's explicit, unticked-by-default agreement that Datorix may use their details to respond to their onboarding request.

**EULA**
The End User License Agreement governing use of the Datorix software. It is accepted when the software is installed or a trial is activated, not when onboarding is requested.

**Privacy Policy**
The published statement of what personal data Datorix collects, why, how long it is kept, and the rights prospects have over it.
